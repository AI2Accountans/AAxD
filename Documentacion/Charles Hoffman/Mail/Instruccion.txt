What is becoming increasingly clear here is that we seem to have three different “planes” or “levels”.

1.  Granular plane/level (Business event journal, Peppol, UBL, XBRL GL, REA, ACTUS)
2.  Mezzanine plane/level (accounting working papers, audit working papers, “report” or other projection; intermediate plane/level where there are aspects of both and a transition between the two)
3.  Aggregated plane/level (Financial statement, analysis model, tax return, compliance report; US GAAP and IFRS Taxonomy)

Key to getting all this to work effectively is to understand that it is a platform and an ecosystem: https://digitalfinancialreporting.blogspot.com/2026/07/platforms-and-ecosystems.html

Part of the business aspect of this is breaking down the silos that impede Accounting & Audit by Design (A&AD).
