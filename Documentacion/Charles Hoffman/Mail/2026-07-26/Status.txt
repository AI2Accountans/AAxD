I am also trying to “bridge the gap” a bit. What I have done in the following blog post, Standards for Interconnecting the Enterprise Stack, is to try and describe the “moving puzzle pieces” of standards and frameworks including UBL, REA, XBRL, SBVR, BPMN, ACTUS and help people grasp that (a) connecting all this stuff is very, very possible and (b) point out how utterly absurd it is to have a bunch of siloed standards that don’t talk to each other well. Yes, you can “map” between all these standards as Richard has done; but another way is to put the pieces together properly.
