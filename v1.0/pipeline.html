A&AD: Going Concern Onboarding Pipeline

Visual methodology for initializing a Semantic Digital Twin in an active enterprise (Going Concern), anchoring deterministic trust from Moment 0 through to the Operational Knowledge Graph.

1

Moment 0

Opening Balance Sheet structured in XBRL GL as the foundational immutable holon.

2

Legal Context

DataBooks containing active contracts and Accounting Policies (Ontologies/SKOS) dictating the rules.

3

Assurance Anchor

The Independent Auditor's Report cryptographically seals the opening balance, securing the baseline.

4

Historical Hydration

Massive ingestion and semantic transmutation of legacy ERP journal entries from Moment 0 to present.

5

Target State

TerminusDB/DFRNT instance deployed, enabling multidimensional QOWL queries and AI Zero-shot auditing.
